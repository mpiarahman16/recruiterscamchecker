Before you reply: verify in 5 minutes

  1. Go to the company yourself. Find the job on the company's own careers page, not through a link they sent.
  2. Ask for an email from the company domain (name@company.com). Real recruiters can do this; scammers stall.
  3. Check that they work there. Open the company's LinkedIn page, go to People, and search for their name.
  4. Reverse-search the photo with Google Lens or TinEye. Stock or AI-generated faces are a common sign.
  5. Look at "About this profile." If they joined recently, aren't verified, and changed the photo recently, be suspicious.
  6. Never share your SSN, bank details, or ID scans before a signed offer. Never pay for equipment or training, and never deposit a check they send.

To report a fake: on their profile, open "…" and choose Report. In the US you can also report at reportfraud.ftc.gov.
